× Definition of Terms

Gear Inches

A traditional measure representing the equivalent diameter of a direct-drive wheel. Higher numbers = harder resistance.

Formula: (Chainring ÷ Cog) × Tyre Diameter

Cadence / Speed

Calculates your ground speed based on your pedaling rate (revolutions per minute). Efficiency is 80–95 RPM.

Speed: (GI × π × Cadence × 60) ÷ 63,360

Cross-Chaining

Highlighted values represent extreme chain angles (Big-to-Big or Small-to-Small). Values are faded and italicized to indicate these gears are mechanically inefficient, cause excessive wear and should be avoided.

Drivetrain Capacity

The total 'chain slack' managed by the rear derailleur. Critical for determining the required rear derailleur cage length.

Formula: (Big Ring - Small Ring) + (Big Cog - Small Cog)

Total Gear Range

The span between your easiest climbing gear and hardest sprint gear. A higher % means a wider range of gears and more versatility.

Formula: (Highest Gear Ratio ÷ Lowest Gear Ratio) × 100%
