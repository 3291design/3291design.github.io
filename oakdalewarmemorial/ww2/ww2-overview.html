Oakdale and WWII
We honor the supreme sacrifice of Oakdale’s fallen heroes by mapping their remarkable journeys across three key areas.
WWII: The Fallen
Our digital roster honors the twenty-four servicemen from Oakdale who gave their lives during World War II. Each individual profile record preserves their identity, military rank, regimental unit, service history, medal verifications, and final resting places across global Commonwealth war cemeteries and local Chapelyards.

WWII: Stories
Explore six thematic chapters mapping the global scale of World War II through the operational context of Oakdale’s servicemen. From the air battles over Europe and defense of the Home Front to grueling campaigns in Italy, the Mediterranean, the jungles of Burma, and the final advance into Germany, these narratives connect our local history to the global conflict.
