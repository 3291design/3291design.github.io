The Soldiers of Oakdale - World War II
A six-part chronicle tracing the global theaters, home front sacrifices, operational aircrews, and the ultimate price paid by the men of Oakdale.
A Global Conflict
The Second World War was a conflict of unprecedented scale, drawing the young men of the Sirhowy Valley away from their tight-knit mining communities and into theatres of war spanning the globe. Unlike the linear trench warfare of their fathers' generation, this war was fought simultaneously across vastly different environments—from the freezing night skies over occupied Europe and the treacherous convoy routes of the Mediterranean, to the blistering deserts of North Africa and the unforgiving jungles of Burma.

To accurately reflect this overlapping reality, our history is organized into six distinct Parts. Each section focuses on a specific operational command or geographic theatre, honouring how Oakdale’s servicemen fought, endured, and ultimately gave their lives across the diverse battlefields of a truly world war.
