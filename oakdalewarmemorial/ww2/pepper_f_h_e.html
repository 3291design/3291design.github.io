Frederick Hugh Edwin Pepper was born in 1917 to Hugh and Olive Pepper. He later married Christianna M. Pepper.
Phaleron War Cemetery, Greece
CWGC Commemorative Site
Frederick entered the British Army and joined the Royal Regiment of Artillery, where he rose to the rank of Sergeant. Following the formal end of hostilities in Europe, Sergeant Pepper was deployed to the Mediterranean theatre. He served with the Central Mediterranean Force, and his unit was sent to Greece to help stabilise the region and maintain order during a highly volatile post-war political climate.
While stationed on active duty in Greece, Sergeant Pepper contracted a severe and aggressive illness. He was diagnosed with miliary tuberculosis, a dangerous form of the disease where bacteria travel through the bloodstream and form tiny lesions across multiple vital organs. Despite the best available military medical intervention, he succumbed to the illness on 24 March 1946 at the age of 29.
Because his death occurred while on active post-war deployment, Sergeant Pepper was buried overseas in the Phaleron War Cemetery (view listing: 961901) in Athens, Greece. His name remains proudly honoured within the local history of Oakdale, keeping his memory alive for the generations that follow on the Oakdale War Memorial.
