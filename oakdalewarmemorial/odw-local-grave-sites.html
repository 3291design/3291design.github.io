Local War Graves & Resting Places
Fifteen servicemen from both World Wars were brought home or succumbed to service-related illness in South Wales. They now rest in three historic local churchyards near our village.
While many men from Oakdale fell on overseas battlefields, fifteen local servicemen lie buried in the Sirhowy Valley. Their graves are officially registered and cared for by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission (CWGC). Click on any soldier's name below to view their profile summary and service history.

Jerusalem Chapelyard • Penmaen Chapelyard • St. David's Churchyard

Jerusalem Chapelyard (Woodfieldside)
Located in Woodfieldside near Blackwood, Jerusalem Chapelyard contains eight registered Commonwealth War Graves—two from World War I and six from World War II. Unfortunately, this historic burial ground is currently closed to the public due to drainage issues, uneven ground, and overgrowth.

Penmaen Congregational Chapelyard
Situated on the hillside overlooking the Sirhowy Valley, Penmaen Chapelyard contains five war graves—two from World War I and three from World War II.

St. David's Churchyard (Cwrt-y-Bella)
