The Soldiers of Oakdale
A four-part chronicle of the major campaigns fought, the conditions endured, and how the men of Oakdale made the ultimate sacrifice.
Chapter I
The Western Front
The Monmouthshire Regiment
Photo credit: Cwm & Waunlwyd Archive
Collieries emptied as local men answered the call, marching shoulder-to-shoulder straight into the brutal slaughter of the Western Front. From the early defense of Ypres and the muddy trenches of the Somme to the final push across the Hindenburg Line, this section follows the grueling path of the Monmouthshire battalions. ► Read more...
Chapter II
Beyond the Western Front
Battle of Shaik Saad
Photo credit: National Army Museum
Far beyond the muddy trenches of France and Flanders, Welsh valley lads were thrust into entirely new and different global theaters. Facing scorching desert heat, treacherous terrain, and severe disease, these campaigns carried men from the colliery towns into the unforgiving landscapes of Gallipoli, Salonika, and Mesopotamia. ► Read more...
Chapter III
Prisoners & The Missing
British POWs
Photo credit: The Past
The chaos of sudden retreats amidst ongoing battlefronts left families waiting at home in agonizing uncertainty. The denial of a known grave or the quiet endurance of imprisonment behind enemy barbed wire created a unique heartache throughout the Valleys, as loved ones awaited telegrams that often brought more questions than closure. ► Read more...
Chapter IV
The Return Home
Evacuation of the wounded
Photo credit: The History Press
Survival on the battlefield did not always guarantee peace or a return to normal life. Returning soldiers often faced grueling medical journeys back to the safety of Wales and the Sirhowy Valley — where physical wounds, psychological trauma, and lingering illness often proved fatal long after the guns finally fell silent across Europe. ► Read more...
