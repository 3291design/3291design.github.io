Fields of Remembrance

The men of Oakdale who fell during the Great War rest in cemeteries across the Western Front and beyond. This directory groups our soldiers by their final resting places.

Merville Communal Cemetery

Merville, France
CWGC Maintained
Merville is a town in the Department of the Nord, France. The communal cemetery was used by medical units and fighting troops from the early months of the war until the German advance in the spring of 1918.

Oakdale Men Commemorated Here:
  • Private John T. Williams (21985) - 11th Btn South Wales Borderers

Ypres (Menin Gate) Memorial

Ypres, Belgium
Memorial to the Missing
The Menin Gate is one of four memorials to the missing in Belgian Flanders which cover the area known as the Ypres Salient. It bears the names of more than 54,000 soldiers who died before 16 August 1917 and have no known grave.
