Dardanelles Campaign
A naval operation led by British and French warships. The goal was to force a passage through the Dardanelles Strait, controlled by the Ottoman Empire and open a sea route to Russia. Failing to break through,the Allied command realized naval power alone would not suffice.
The Dardanelles Campaign was the primary operation that dramatically shifted the scale of World War I beyond the Western Front, turning a deadlocked European conflict into a truly global war.
